**Story mechanics that actually matter**
- Frame 1 alone won't convert. The link sticker goes on **frame 3**, but add a second sticker on frame 1 too: a third of people never reach the last frame.

- Add a **question sticker** on frame 2: "What do you check before you trust a coin?" Free research, and every answer is a warm DM you can follow up.
- Post between 19:00–22:00 local. Re-post frames 1 + 3 two days later; overlap on story viewers is low.
- Save the set as a **Highlight** called "TESTERS" so it keeps recruiting after 24h.

## 5. What not to say

These words read as "scam" to anyone who's spent time in crypto Instagram, and some are legal risks:

**buy · sell · signal · signals group · call · entry · target · guaranteed · profit · x10 · moon · pump · passive income · financial freedom · trading bot · AI predicts the price**

Your differentiator is the opposite of all of that: *it shows its work, keeps receipts, and doesn't promise anything.* Lead with the transparency and the "not financial advice" line. It builds more trust than any performance claim would.
