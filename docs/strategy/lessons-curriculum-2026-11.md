# Qirat lessons: the step-by-step course

Written 14 November 2026 for Abdelkerim.

**Status:** all 22 lessons are written in English, French and light Arabic, on the branch `claude/lessons-curriculum` (not pushed). Before release:
1. Read the Arabic: `docs/learn/darija-review.md` has 865 lines to tick.
2. Try the Learn page on your phone.
3. Merge.

**Language (your decision, 14 Nov 2026):** the lessons' Arabic is light Arabic, simple enough for readers from Morocco to the Gulf, not Tunisian Darija. Trading words are in Arabic, with the English term in brackets the first time a lesson uses them. Digits are Western (12, 500, 0.1%). The rest of the app and the videos stay in Darija for now.

The goal you set: lessons that go step by step (basics, then intermediate, then advanced), with no empty lessons and no false facts, checked online.

The rules stay the same:
- education and practice only
- no "buy this" or "sell now", no signals, no profit promises
- no "how to buy crypto from Tunisia"
- crypto and trading only

## 1. How good courses are built (what I copied)

| Source | What it does | What Qirat takes from it |
|---|---|---|
| Binance Academy | Beginner track: blockchain → crypto → trading basics → strategies. Short videos, one quiz after each. Articles tagged Beginner, Intermediate or Advanced, with "Key takeaways". | Three levels, a quiz after every lesson, a short takeaway |

| CFPB and OECD guidance on financial education | Small steps, information right when it is needed, practice, make the good choice easy | Each lesson points to the live app: the practice wallet, the coin's risk, the weather |

**Why this order:**
- Risk and "only what you can lose" come before any trading.
- Reading a chart comes before placing orders.
- Leverage and indicators come last, as most good courses do.
- It also follows the six modules of the «قيراط بقيراط» video series, so each episode can point to its lesson.

**Lesson size:** 2 to 3 minutes, 4 to 6 short sections, one takeaway and 3 quiz questions, as today.

**What changes in the app:**
- The Learn page groups lessons under "Level 1 · Basics", "Level 2 · Intermediate" and "Level 3 · Advanced".
- Each lesson page shows its level.
- The Telegram `/learn` lesson of the day uses the same list. Today it has 5 lessons while the app has 6, so the bot and the app can show different lessons.
- Your karat (10K → 24K) keeps working: it scales with the number of lessons and never drops.

**Your review work:** read the Arabic of all 22 lessons, the glossary and the Learn screen, about 865 lines in `docs/learn/darija-review.md`. It is grouped by level; allow 5 to 10 minutes a lesson. To regenerate it after an edit, run `npm run learn:darija` in `frontend`.

## 3. Audit of the 6 written lessons

### 1 · Bitcoin

Correct overall. Bitcoin's whitepaper is dated 31 Oct 2008 and its first block 3 Jan 2009. The cap is 21 million (exactly 20,999,999.9769). One satoshi is 0.00000001 BTC.

Fixes:
- "Copied on thousands of computers" is true: about 19,000 reachable nodes (btcnodes.io, Nov 2026).
- "Thousands of other coins" undersells it: CoinGecko lists over 22,000. "Many disappear" is backed by CoinGecko research: 53% of tokens listed since 2021 are no longer traded.

### 2 · USDT

Correct overall.

Fixes:
- Add that Tether's reserves are checked by an accounting firm's attestation, not a full audit, and are mostly US Treasury bills.
- Add that in 2021 two US authorities (the CFTC and New York's attorney general) fined Tether for saying USDT was always fully backed when it wasn't.
- The dips below $1 were: about $0.87–0.95 depending on the exchange on 15 Oct 2018, and about $0.92–0.95 on 12 May 2022.
- The "dinar side" section stays a warning, not a how-to.

### 3 · Price swings

Matches the engine:
- "Typical day" is the 30-day standard deviation of daily moves.
- "Rough month" is a 1-in-20 month under a normal model.
- The risk grade is the coin's rank in thirds among the 18 tracked coins.

Fix: "±2% on a normal day" now says most days move less than that and some move more.

### 4 · Candles

Matches the chart: 30 daily candles from Binance. One fact added: a day runs from 01:00 to 01:00 Tunisian time (00:00 UTC), and the last candle is still forming. The quiz is unchanged, because this week's videos use its question 3.

### 5 · Scams

Mostly correct. It stays one lesson, focused on crypto.

Fixes:
- "Check the name against the CMF's warnings": the CMF has no crypto-specific list. Its alerts are general (latest 27 Oct 2026). Now: "search for the name with the word arnaque, and check the CMF's alerts".
- The "P2P meetups turned into robberies" line becomes "there have been reports in Tunisia of...", since there is no single official source.

### 6 · FOMO

Fixes:
- It said the market weather measures the crowd's mood. **Wrong:** the weather is Bitcoin against its 50-day and 200-day averages. Extreme greed only turns "calm" into "choppy".
- "When greed is extreme, pullbacks have tended to come more often" is not backed by Qirat's own study. **Removed.** Now: extreme greed means many people already bought; it does not say when the price turns.
- "See lesson 3" breaks when lessons move. Lesson numbers are no longer used inside the text.
- Lesson subtitle: "lessons on crypto, scams and risk" becomes "crypto and trading, step by step".

### Outlines (not yet shown as lessons)

- **Law:** "The BCT said in 2023 that crypto is not money" is **wrong as written**. That sentence comes from a *draft* exchange code reported by Managers on 25 Feb 2023, not from a BCT statement. I found no primary text for the 2017–2018 "BCT ban" quoted on many blogs, so it is left out.
- **Wallets:** "12 or 24 words" becomes "usually 12 or 24" (the standard allows 12–24).
