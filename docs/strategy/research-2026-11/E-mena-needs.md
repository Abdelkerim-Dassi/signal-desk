## 1. What is new since 2026-11-07 (headline findings)

1. **Tunisia is moving on two tracks at once: legalisation talk and tighter enforcement.**
   - The draft **Code des changes** (filed Oct 2025, backed by 50+ MPs) is described as the first Tunisian text to recognise and regulate digital currencies; the ARP finance committee was still holding hearings in Apr–Jun 2026 and no final vote has been found. [Mosaïque FM, 23 Dec 2025](https://www.mosaiquefm.net/mobile/ar/أخبار-مال-أعمال-اقتصاد-تونس/1489309/الكتاري-مشروع-مجلة-الصرف-يعترف-بالعملات-الرقمية-وينظم-التعامل-معها) · [La Presse, 4 Jun 2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/06/04/reglementation-de-change-un-nouveau-code-a-lexamen/) · [Mosaïque FM, hearing](https://www.mosaiquefm.net/mobile/ar/تونس-وطنية/1515245/جلسة-استماع-حول-مقترح-قانون-إصدار-مجلة-الصرف)
   - Meanwhile **Tunisian customs agents were trained by France's Onaf (late Oct 2026) to trace and seize crypto**. [Kapitalis, 5 Nov 2026](https://kapitalis.com/tunisie/?p=19935366)
   - **BCT Circular 2026-10 (25 Sep 2026)** caps e-wallet balances (TND 1,500 / 5,000 / 20,000 by tier), caps individual cash remittances at TND 3,000, logs everything for 10 years, enforcement from late Dec 2026. That squeezes the wallet rails (Flouci, D17 etc.) that P2P sellers use. [LaunchBase Africa, 28 Sep 2026](https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/09/28/tunisias-fintech/)
   - BCT **Circular 2/2026** adds AML duties for exchange offices; a Jan 2026 judicial study calls crypto "an ideal tool" for laundering. [AllAfrica/TAP, 29 Jan 2026](https://allafrica.com/stories/202601300069.html)
2. **Tunisia jumped from 80th to 52nd in Chainalysis' 2025 global adoption index** (43rd for retail centralised services), despite the ban; growth is attributed to informal/P2P activity. [Tuniscope, 28 Oct 2025](https://www.tuniscope.com/article/416261/business/economie/cryptomonnaies-231311)
3. **A new Tunisian scam type: "crypto sale" meetups that are armed robberies.** A Manouba ring lured young men via social media with cheap crypto offers, then robbed them at knife-point (one victim lost TND 30,000). [La Presse, 5 Apr 2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/04/05/arnaque-a-la-cryptomonnaie-a-la-manouba-une-femme-a-la-tete-dun-reseau-criminel-demantelearnaque-a-la-cryptomonnaie-a-la-manouba-une-femme-a-la-tete-dun-reseau-criminel-demantele/)
4. **Arabic crypto education is being bought up by exchanges, and none of it is in Darija.** Rain acquired Arabic media firm Digital Ma'arefa (Apr 2026) ([Forbes ME, 8 Oct 2026](https://www.forbesmiddleeast.com/lists/the-middle-easts-fintech-50-2026/rain/)); Binance Academy × Bitcoineen run a **Modern Standard Arabic**, UAE-focused cohort programme (500 seats) ([Zawya](https://www.zawya.com/en/press-release/companies-news/binance-academy-launches-first-fully-arabic-program-with-bitcoineen-ntc7ajbp)). In Tunisian crypto Telegram ads, colloquial Darija is "rare, est. under 10%" of creatives. [TGAdsSpy, Apr–Jul 2026 (marketing)](https://tgadsspy.com/blog/tunisia-telegram-ads-crypto-mena-2026)
5. **Crypto investors lean on AI for research.** In France, 33% of crypto investors use AI before investing (vs 19% of stock investors); Finary now lets Claude/ChatGPT/Mistral read a user's portfolio read-only. [AMF special edition, 8 Jun 2026](https://www.amf-france.org/fr/actualites-publications/communiques/communiques-de-lamf/edition-speciale-du-barometre-amf-encore-peu-utilisee-dans-les-pratiques-dinvestissement) · [France Transactions](https://www.francetransactions.com/actus/news-epargne/finary-connecte-a-claude.html)
6. **Halal-crypto screeners exist but are English-first and paywalled.** HalalScreener (EN/AR, $9.99/mo), Sharlife (EN only, $12.99/mo or $4.99 crypto unlock), Saraf (EN only). None serve French or Darija.

## 2. How Tunisians actually get and use crypto (2025–2026)

### 2.1 Legal status: banned, but under debate
- Crypto has been banned since 2018 (BCT directive); use stays illegal and unregulated. [Tuniscope, Oct 2025](https://www.tuniscope.com/article/416261/business/economie/cryptomonnaies-231311). A crypto-vendor explainer says penalties go up to fines and 5 years' prison; the page has commercial bias and cites secondary sources. [Lightspark (marketing)](https://www.lightspark.com/knowledge/is-crypto-legal-in-tunisia)
- The finance minister said (Jan 2025) the draft code would let citizens hold and trade crypto; a preliminary text quoted by Managers says the BCT does not see crypto-assets as money, so the final wording is uncertain. [Search summary of AllAfrica Jan 2025 and Managers](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202501060133.html) (the AllAfrica page returned 403 to direct fetch)
- The bill was filed in Oct 2025 by MP Maher Ktari with 40+ (later 50+) MPs; committee review began 23 Feb 2026. [Tunisie Numérique](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/un-nouveau-code-des-changes-en-tunisie-bientot-soumis-a-larp/) · [Mosaïque FM](https://www.mosaiquefm.net/mobile/ar/أخبار-مال-أعمال-اقتصاد-تونس/1489309/الكتاري-مشروع-مجلة-الصرف-يعترف-بالعملات-الرقمية-وينظم-التعامل-معها)
- A Mar 2026 debate article: customs worry about competence moving to the public prosecutor. [Business News, 21 Apr 2026](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/04/21/reforme-du-code-des-changes-la-douane-sinquiete-dun-eventuel-transfert-de-competence-vers-le-ministere-public/1397817/)
- **e-dinar / CBDC:** the BCT has repeatedly denied issuing a CBDC (the 2019 "e-dinar" story was refuted). No 2025–26 BCT CBDC study found. Morocco, by contrast, launched an **e-Dirham pilot on 14 May 2026**. [Ledger Insights](https://www.ledgerinsights.com/tunisian-central-bank-refutes-e-dinar-news/) · [Tunisie Numérique](https://www.tunisienumerique.com/tunisie-adoption-par-la-banque-centrale-dune-monnaie-numerique-precisions-de-la-bct/) · [Wafir.ma](https://wafir.ma/fr/actu/e-dirham-cbdc-bam-pilote-2026-monnaie-numerique-banque-centrale-test-utilisateurs)

**Product implication:** Qirat must keep a "education and information, not a broker" stance and show a clear "legal status in Tunisia" explainer, updated as the code moves.

### 2.3 Rails people use
- **Binance P2P / Noones + local transfers.** P2P USDT remittance channels are the most Tunisia-specific crypto ad category on Telegram; pitches stress speed, a better rate than bank wires and no bank account needed. USDT is called "دولار رقمي" (digital dollar). [TGAdsSpy (marketing)](https://tgadsspy.com/blog/tunisia-telegram-ads-crypto-mena-2026)
- Search summaries of a buying guide describe Tunisians cashing out USDT via Binance P2P to Flouci, D17, M2M and bank accounts, and worrying that repeated transfers get flagged. [newhedge.io (affiliate; page 403 on fetch)](https://newhedge.io/buy/tunisia)
- **Wallet rails just got tighter:** BCT Circular 2026-10 caps (see §1). [LaunchBase Africa](https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/09/28/tunisias-fintech/). Since May 2026, Flouci, D17, e-Dinar and others share the BCT-backed **TUNPAY** brand. [Business News, 13 Mar 2026 (via search summary)](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/03/13/diaspora-tunisienne-pres-de-9-millions-de-dinars-envoyes-en-2025-le-digital-transforme-les-habitudes/1391957/)
- **Freelancers:** PayPal in Tunisia is "send only". An Aug 2026 bill (11 MPs) would open international payment platforms but with a **15% withdrawal levy, a $5,000/month cap and forced conversion to dinars**, which critics say will push money back to informal channels. [Business News, 10 Aug 2026](https://businessnews.com.tn/2026/08/10/freelances-la-loi-qui-veut-ouvrir-paypal-et-preleve-15/1413684/). A blog says some freelancers already take USDT/USDC. [Jobbers.io (marketing)](https://www.jobbers.io/tunisias-freelance-economy-2026-why-zero-commission-platforms-are-growing-340/)
- **Diaspora:** official remittances were TND 1,467.7m in Jan–Feb 2026 (+18.2%) and over TND 3.6bn by mid-June 2026; ~1.3m Tunisians abroad. [La Presse, 13 Mar 2026](https://www.lapresse.tn/2026/03/13/pres-de-15-milliard-de-dinars-transferes-par-les-tunisiens-de-letranger-en-deux-mois/) · [AllAfrica, Jun 2026](https://fr.allafrica.com/stories/202606150199.html) · [TGAdsSpy](https://tgadsspy.com/blog/tunisia-telegram-ads-crypto-mena-2026)
- **RedotPay:** RedotPay's help centre lists Tunisia as **not supported as a mailing address** for physical cards (billing addresses are OK), so Tunisians are limited to virtual cards. Fees reported: $10 virtual card, 1% conversion, 2% ATM. [RedotPay help centre](https://helpcenter.redotpay.com/ar/articles/14254838-قيود-إصدار-البطاقة) · [Penchan (3rd party)](https://penchan.co/cards/tn/redotpay/)
- **Cash economy context:** 30–40% of activity is informal/cash. [LaunchBase Africa](https://launchbaseafrica.com/2026/09/28/tunisias-fintech/)

## 4. Scam landscape (2025–2026)

### 4.1 Global numbers
- Chainalysis 2026 Crime Report: **~$17bn** scam losses in 2025 (≥$14bn on-chain), impersonation scams **+1,400%** YoY, AI-linked operations earn **4.5×** more per campaign ($3.2m vs $719k), and the average scam payment rose from $782 to **$2,764**. [Decrypt, 14 Jan 2026](https://decrypt.co/354624/ai-impersonation-drove-crypto-scam-losses-record-17-billion-2025-chainalysis) · [Infosecurity Magazine](https://www.infosecurity-magazine.com/news/impersonation-fraud-record-17bn/)
- In France, a CryptoActu reading of the AMF barometer says **29% of crypto investors** report having been scammed on an investment (vs 16% of all French people; 32% of under-35s). This is self-reported and from the press, not the AMF's own release. [CryptoActu](https://cryptoactu.com/enquetes/etudiants-francais-memecoins-solana/)

## 5. Practice / paper trading and gamified learning

- **Binance demo trading:** one demo for spot and futures with $5,000 spot and $16,800 futures virtual funds, usable before KYC, but under the same regional rules as real trading. [Bitcoin.com News](https://news.bitcoin.com/binance-mock-trading-feature-offers-5k-virtual-funds-for-beginners-and-advanced-users/) · [Blockchain Reporter](https://blockchainreporter.net/binance-launches-unified-spot-and-futures-demo-trading-to-lower-entry-barriers/)
- **eToro virtual portfolio:** $100,000 virtual money; most cited beginner simulator in "best of 2026" lists (affiliate). [CoinSutra](https://coinsutra.com/crypto-paper-trading-apps-simulators/)
- **OKX, Phemex, TradingView, Bitget mock trading** also appear repeatedly (affiliate lists). [CoinGape](https://coingape.com/cryptocurrency-exchanges/best-crypto-paper-trading/) · [Bitget](https://www.bitget.cloud/academy/mock-trading-2026)
- **Evidence on outcomes is weak:** no rigorous study shows paper trading improves live results; commentators warn it can build overconfidence because no money is at risk. Barber & Odean's work shows overconfident traders trade more and earn less. [Alpaca](https://alpaca.markets/learn/paper-trading-vs-live-trading-a-data-backed-guide-on-when-to-start-trading-real-money) · [academic paper (CORE)](https://core.ac.uk/download/227107775.pdf)
- **MENA proof that guided practice works:** Thndr Alpha (guided beginner allocation and education) reached 438,879 users; 76% of Thndr's 2025 users were new to investing (avg age 30); Thndr Learn includes a stock simulator. [Thndr blog](https://thndr.app/blogpost/thndr-alpha-your-first-step-into-investing/) · [Amwal Al Ghad](https://en.amwalalghad.com/?p=207143)
- **Gamified finance (Zogo):** 2m+ users since 2018 via 200+ US financial institutions; 94% self-reported higher confidence; retention claims are vendor-published only. [Zogo](https://zogo.com/press/zogo-360-is-here) · [Global Fintech Series](https://globalfintechseries.com/analytics/zogo-is-quietly-powering-a-shift-in-how-financial-institutions-increase-retention-through-in-app-learning/)
- **Retention reality check:** finance apps keep ~4.5% of users at day 30 (AppsFlyer via Business of Apps; other sources range 3–17%). [Business of Apps](https://www.businessofapps.com/?p=98027) · [eMarketer](https://www.emarketer.com/content/finance-apps-keep-users-engaged-longer-than-any-other-industry)

**Implication:** a Qirat "practice wallet" should be spot-only, have no leverage, track behaviour (e.g. "you bought after a +30% day") and tie into the Karat/risk grades, so it teaches calm habits instead of rewarding gambling. Exchange demos already cover the "learn the order book" use case.

## 7. Implications for Qirat product strategy (short)

1. **Make "Scam check" the second pillar next to Karat grades.** Inputs: coin/token address (GoPlus free tier), link/domain (ScamSniffer list + MetaMask list), platform name (curated CMF/AMMC/BCLCC list plus community reports), and pasted message text (LLM red-flag scorer trained on the AMMC 6-step pattern, "AI bot 100%/month", "unlock fee", "activation pack", "recruit 2 friends"). Output in Darija/FR/AR. Free, because it builds trust and drives shares.
2. **Publish a living "Crypto & the law in Tunisia" page** (code des changes status, BCT circulars 2026-2 and 2026-10, customs training) with no advice to break rules. It positions Qirat as the neutral source before legalisation and is useful PR when the vote happens.
3. **Darija is the moat.** Every Arabic competitor found is MSA or English; Gulf exchanges are buying Arabic media. Lean on the Darija voice and glossary already planned.
4. **Pro-tier anchors:** halal screeners at $5–17/mo and Finary at €150/yr suggest Pro features could be AI Q&A in Darija, the practice wallet with behaviour coaching, Shariah notes depth and alerts. Keep scam check and grades free.
5. **Avoid** P2P rate boards, broker referrals and "how to cash out" tooling. Legal exposure is rising (customs, CTAF, wallet caps), and it would also undercut the neutrality brand.
