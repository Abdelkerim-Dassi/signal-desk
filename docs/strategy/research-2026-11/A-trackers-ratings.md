# A. Trackers, ratings and research platforms: competitor scan

Prepared 2026-11-08 for Qirat (qirat.tn) product strategy. Scope: 11 competitors, covering what each offers, its prices, what users praise and dislike, languages, and gaps for a Tunisian/MENA beginner. Also: which ones publish a verifiable track record, and regulatory or legal history.

**Method and caveats**
- Sources are official pricing pages and App Store listings fetched 2026-11-08, Trustpilot pages fetched the same day, plus news and third-party reviews (dated where known).
- App Store in-app-purchase (IAP) price lists combine several storefronts and older SKUs. Where IAP prices conflict with the web pricing page, both are shown.
- Trustpilot samples are small and skewed toward complaints. Trustpilot itself flags several of these profiles as unclaimed or not inviting reviews. Use them as a sign of what people complain about, not how often.
- Quotes are kept under 15 words.
- The web-search budget ran out before I could check two things: Tunisian card and payment restrictions, and any 2026 Delta AI features. Both are marked "not verified" below.

## 1. CoinStats

**Offer**
- Portfolio tracker that syncs exchanges and wallets, with custom alerts, a crypto API and an MCP server. ([pricing](https://coinstats.app/pricing/))
- **AI:** the CoinStats AI Agent went into public beta in April 2026 for Premium and Degen users on web, iOS and Android. It has three modes ("Deep Research", "Backtesting" and "Fast Mode") and can analyse the user's portfolio. ([Ambcrypto, Apr 2026](https://ambcrypto.com/coinstats-ai-agent-outperforms-gemini-claude-and-chatgpt-in-open-source-crypto-deep-research-benchmark/), [BeInCrypto](https://beincrypto.com/coinstats-ceo-discusses-ai-agent-for-crypto-research/))
- CoinStats says the agent scored 79/100 on its own open-source research benchmark, ahead of Gemini (67), ChatGPT (61) and Claude (58). This is a vendor benchmark scored by an AI judge. ([letsdatascience](https://letsdatascience.com/news/coinstats-agent-outperforms-general-purpose-models-on-crypto-98b3ab1c))
- No coin ratings or grades.

**Pricing**

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Basic | Free | 10 portfolios, 20,000 transactions |
| Premium | $13.99/mo after trial (pricing page); $15.99/mo month-to-month per third parties | 100 portfolios, up to 100k transactions, AI Agent + Deep Research |
| Degen | $89/mo, or about $62.91/mo billed annually | 500 portfolios, more AI credits, AI Backtesting, VIP support |
| Team | Custom | — |
| Lifetime | Premium $399 one-time; Degen $2,600 one-time | — |

- Sources: [coinstats.app/pricing](https://coinstats.app/pricing/), [ComparEdge](https://comparedge.com/tools/coinstats/pricing).
- The US App Store lists IAPs from $4.99 to $164.99 and notes prices vary by country. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coinstats-crypto-portfolio/id1247849330))
- There is a 7-day trial. The pricing page itself also says "14-day money-back", which is inconsistent. ([pricing](https://coinstats.app/pricing/))

**Praise**
- App Store: 4.8★ from 86K ratings. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coinstats-crypto-portfolio/id1247849330))
- Trustpilot: 4.2/5 from 8,821 reviews, and the company replies to 96% of negative reviews. ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/coinstats.app))
- Quotes:
  - "Clean interface, quick syncing, and accurate data" (Trustpilot, Oct 2025)
  - "the best portfolio tracker that i have came across" (App Store)

**Complaints**
- Wrong numbers:
  - "Profit/Loss and cost-basis figures in my portfolio were clearly incorrect" (Trustpilot, Sep 2026)
  - "Some cost averages are incorrect" (App Store)
- Billing and trials:
  - "I was charged A$125.03 for another subscription period" (Sep 2026)
  - A missed trial-end reminder (Oct 2026)
- Data loss: a paid portfolio was "deleted for no reason and with no warning" (Sep 2026). ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/coinstats.app))
- Widgets: "widget stopped functioning 2 months ago" (App Store)

**Languages**
- The app supports English plus 12 others, including French, but **not Arabic**. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coinstats-crypto-portfolio/id1247849330))

**Gaps for a Tunisian beginner**
- Built for power users: 100 to 500 portfolios, backtesting.
- No Arabic or Darija.
- No plain-language safety grade per coin.
- AI sits behind a USD subscription.
- Reviews show P&L accuracy is fragile, which matters a lot to beginners.

## 2. Delta (Delta by eToro)

**Offer**
- Multi-asset tracker covering crypto, stocks, funds, FX and commodities.
- Syncs with "over 10,000 brokers, exchanges, banks, and wallets."
- Price alerts, smart notifications, and "Why is it moving?" explanations.
- PRO adds Portfolio Insights, realised and unrealised gains, and unlimited connections. ([eToro Delta page](https://www.etoro.com/investing/delta/))
- No AI feature is described on the page beyond "Why is it moving". I could not check for 2026 AI features because the search budget ran out.
- The free tier covers 10 assets. Tiers are Basic, PRO and PRO+. ([App Store RU listing](https://apps.apple.com/ru/app/delta-%D1%82%D1%80%D0%B5%D0%BA%D0%B5%D1%80-%D0%BA%D1%80%D0%B8%D0%BF%D1%82%D0%BE-%D0%BF%D0%BE%D1%80%D1%82%D1%84%D0%B5%D0%BB%D0%B5%D0%B9/id1288676542))
- eToro bought Delta in November 2019. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/delta-investment-tracker/id1288676542))

**Pricing (US App Store IAPs)**

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| PRO | $6.99 / $10.49 / $13.99 | $58.99 / $99.90 |
| PRO+ | $13.99 | $107.99 |

- Source: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/delta-investment-tracker/id1288676542).
- A December 2025 Benzinga review calls PRO "somewhat costly." ([Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/money/delta-crypto-tracker-by-etoro-review))

**Praise**
- 4.7★ from 11K ratings.
- Quotes:
  - "An app that does what it promises"
  - "the most aesthetically pleasing app" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/delta-investment-tracker/id1288676542))

**Complaints**
- Lost data: "Delta randomly deleted transactions from portfolio" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/delta-investment-tracker/id1288676542))
- Trial billing: a user was charged about €100 the day after the PRO trial ended, with no reminder (Feb 2025).
- Freezes, crashes, and Coinbase sync failures. ([unitQ](https://unitq.com/unitq-scorecards/deltainvestmenttracker))

**Languages**
- English plus 17 others, including French and Turkish, but **not Arabic**. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/delta-investment-tracker/id1288676542))

**Gaps for a Tunisian beginner**
- A pure tracker: no guidance, ratings or education.
- The 10-asset free cap is fine for beginners.
- No Arabic.
- Owned by a broker (eToro), so it can feel like a funnel into eToro.

## 3. CoinGecko

**Offer**
- Market data on more than 10,000 coins, plus a portfolio (synced between web and app), price alerts, large-mover notifications, widgets and a news feed. ([App Store search summary](https://apps.apple.com/app/1390323960))
- **AI:**
  - Gecko AI is a conversational research chat over CoinGecko's data. ([Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/coingecko-launches-gecko-ai-research-tool/))
  - On 2026-04-22 CoinGecko launched three more tools ([Bitcoin.com, Apr 2026](https://news.bitcoin.com/coingecko-launches-ai-market-intelligence-tools-and-unified-partner-platform-for-crypto-projects/)):
    - Market Insights: an AI explanation of why prices moved.
    - Advanced Charts.
    - Portfolio Insights: read-only wallet P&L, average buy price and allocation, EVM chains only for now.
- No safety or quality grade for retail users.

**Pricing**

| Plan | Price | What you get |
|---|---|---|
| Free | $0 | 7 Gecko AI uses (one-time), 500 manual transactions, 2 wallets |
| Premium | $10/mo, or $99.90/yr (~$8.32/mo) | 100 Gecko AI uses, unlimited manual transactions, 100 wallets, AI-powered insights, no ads |

- Source: [CoinGecko Premium pricing](https://www.coingecko.com/en/premium/pricing).
- The App Store price is $9.90/mo. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/1390323960))

**Praise**
- App Store: 4.8★ from 27K ratings.
- Quote: "This app has a tremendous amount of information!" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/1390323960))
- Trustpilot (May 2025): "Easily understandable interface." ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/coingecko.com))

**Complaints**
- Trustpilot score is 2.1/5 from 167 reviews, an unclaimed profile.
- Recurring theme: users accuse CoinGecko of listing scam tokens or being paid for rankings.
  - "accepts payment for coin ranking" (Dec 2025)
  - "supports scam chains" (May 2026)
  - Source: [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/coingecko.com)
- App Store: a portfolio "won't bring up" bug (2022, later fixed). ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/1390323960))

**Languages**
- The iOS app is **English only**. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/1390323960))
- The website has an **Arabic** version. ([coingecko.com/ar](https://www.coingecko.com/ar))

**Gaps for a Tunisian beginner**
- Shows everything (17k+ tokens, including scams) with no "should a beginner touch this?" layer.
- The AI is metered: 7 free uses.
- The app is English-only.

## 4. CoinMarketCap (CMC), including CMC AI

**Offer**
- Prices, a read-only portfolio, watchlists, alerts, a community feed, and "diamonds" rewards. ([CMC Academy](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/coinmarketcap-features-track-research-and-build-with-crypto-data))
- **CMC AI (phase 1, May 2025):** pre-generated Q&A on major token pages, built from market, on-chain, news and social data. ([The Block](https://www.theblock.co/press-releases/354872/))
- Price explanations refresh every 30 minutes and during big moves. No login is required. ([CMC AI page](https://coinmarketcap.com/events/cmc-ai/))
- Features listed as "coming soon" with no dates ([CMC AI page](https://coinmarketcap.com/events/cmc-ai/)):
  - A portfolio "Analyze" feature with "suggested adjustments" and short- and long-term recommendations.
  - Token comparisons.
  - Synced chat history.
- CMC AI pages state that AI can make mistakes and that the content is not financial advice. ([CMC Academy](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/article/coinmarketcap-features-track-research-and-build-with-crypto-data))
- **September 2026:** CMC AI endpoints were added to the Pro API, Enterprise-only at launch, with free-tier AI credits "to follow." ([Unchained/Chainwire, Sep 10 2026](https://unchainedcrypto.com/press-release/new-cmc-ai-endpoints-make-coinmarketcaps-industry-leading-api-even-more-powerful/))
- CMC also has an AI Agent Hub (MCP, CLI, x402) in beta. ([CMC Academy](https://coinmarketcap.com/academy/es/article/coinmarketcap-ai-agent-hub-now-live))

**Pricing (US App Store IAPs)**

| Plan | Monthly | Yearly |
|---|---|---|
| Essential | $11.99 | $109.99 |
| Plus | $23.99 | $229.99 |
| Max | $119.99 | — |

- Source: [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coinmarketcap-com/id1282107098).
- I could not confirm what each tier includes.
- The core site and app are free and ad-supported.
- "CMC Max" is also the name of a separate paid product for listed projects (visibility, badges, ad discounts). ([CMC Max](https://coinmarketcap.com/products/cmc-max/))

**Praise**
- App Store: 4.8★ from 173K ratings.
- Quotes:
  - "Super reliable and informative"
  - "Very user-friendly interface" ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coinmarketcap-com/id1282107098))
- Trustpilot:
  - "one of the most reliable places to check market data" (Sep 2026)
  - Praise for warning triangles on unverified market caps (Jan 2026)

**Complaints**
- Trustpilot score is **1.3/5** from 837 reviews. Complaints include ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/coinmarketcap.com)):
  - Paid creator posts: "full of paid posts" (Dec 2025)
  - Bots in the community feed
  - Rewards that are always "Sold Out" (Oct 2026)
  - Listing tokens "that has no community" (Jan 2026)
- Scammers impersonating CMC staff call users ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/coinmarketcap.com)):
  - "called up to 20 times per day" (Aug 2026)
  - Fee-to-withdraw scams (Feb 2026)
- App Store: "It won't stay logged in"; "you can't actually buy, sell or trade crypto". ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coinmarketcap-com/id1282107098))

**Languages**
- English plus 20 others, **including Arabic and French**. ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/coinmarketcap-com/id1282107098))

**Gaps for a Tunisian beginner**
- Arabic exists, but the experience is cluttered with paid promotion and rewards gamification. Owned by Binance (context only, not newly verified here).
- AI "price prediction" content goes against a calm, neutral stance.
- Its brand is a target for impersonation scams. A scam-check feature would answer this exact pain.

- AI integrations let Claude, ChatGPT or Grok agents read the portfolio and reconcile statements. ([kubera.com](https://www.kubera.com/))

**Pricing**

|---|---|---|

- 14-day trial, no free tier. ([kubera.com](https://www.kubera.com/))
- The price has risen from $150 and $199 in earlier years. ([Jean Galea review](https://jeangalea.com/kubera-review), [Mezzi](https://www.mezzi.com/blog/kubera-price-hike-worth-it-alternatives-compared))

**Languages**
- Not stated on the site. It is multi-currency. ([kubera.com](https://www.kubera.com/))

## 6. Token Metrics

**Offer**
- AI ratings on 6,000+ assets: Trader Grade (0–100, short term, hourly) and Investor Grade (long term, daily). Older material also mentions Risk and Fundamental grades. ([Product Hunt API listing](https://www.producthunt.com/p/token-metrics/token-metrics-api), [altFINS comparison, 2026-01-09](https://altfins.com/crypto-news/article/altfins-vs-tokenmetrics-and-why-altfins-can-be-a-strong-alternative))
- AI-managed indices: DeFi, AI, memecoin, RWA and chain-specific baskets.
- "Moonshots" (under-the-radar tokens), a research chatbot, Telegram and Discord bots, and an API with 21 endpoints. ([Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com/p/token-metrics/token-metrics-api), [todaytesting, Jan 2026](https://todaytesting.com/token-metrics-review/))
- The TMAI AI agent on X launched in February 2025. ([Cryptonomist](https://en.cryptonomist.ch/2025/02/11/token-metrics-launches-an-ai-agent-that-provides-automated-trading-analysis-and-insights/))
- A $TMAI token. Trustpilot users praise staking returns. ([todaytesting](https://todaytesting.com/token-metrics-review/), [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tokenmetrics.com))
- An in-app "Trading" feature lets users act on signals directly. ([FF News](https://ffnews.com/newsarticle/cryptocurrency/token-metrics-launches-ai-powered-crypto-trading-for-smarter-faster-transactions/))
- The app sign-in page now pitches "Follow traders with strong records and vaults we've checked", which suggests copy-trading or vaults. ([app.tokenmetrics.com](https://app.tokenmetrics.com/en/pricing))
- A "TM Global 100" index with weekly rebalancing and a public transaction log was in waitlist in October 2025. Launch status is unconfirmed. ([dev.to mirror](https://dev.to/saira_zeeshan_35fc05971fd/crypto-index-transactions-log-why-full-transparency-matters-2025-13l9))

**Pricing (unclear; the official page blocked fetches, HTTP 403)**

| Source | Prices quoted |
|---|---|
| Older aggregators | Basic $19/mo, Advanced $99/mo, Premium $299/mo, VIP $749/mo |
| todaytesting (Jan 2026, affiliate) | Advanced $79/mo or $699/yr; Premium $199/mo or $1,899/yr |
| altFINS (Jan 2026, competitor) | Claims "Does not offer paid plan anymore!" but also cites a $2,000/yr cost |

- Sources: [zoftwarehub](https://zoftwarehub.com/en-ae/products/token-metrics/pricing), [todaytesting](https://todaytesting.com/token-metrics-review/), [altFINS](https://altfins.com/crypto-news/article/altfins-vs-tokenmetrics-and-why-altfins-can-be-a-strong-alternative).
- **Treat all of these as unverified.** The business model appears to be shifting toward the token, trading and vaults.

**Performance claims**
- Marketing says Smart Indices are "outperforming manual portfolio management". No audited returns, benchmark, period or methodology are given. ([search summary of affiliated blogs](https://dev.to/crypto-trader/how-token-metrics-users-beat-exchange-traders-with-ai-backed-indices-18h8))
- The affiliate review cites no returns or win rates. ([todaytesting](https://todaytesting.com/token-metrics-review/))
- **No independent, loss-inclusive track record of grades or signals was found.**

**Praise (Trustpilot)**
- "Clear even for a beginner" (Aug 2025, verified)
- "They never stop enhancing" (Apr 2025)
- Source: [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tokenmetrics.com)

**Complaints**
- Trustpilot score is **2.4/5** from 118 reviews; the company does not reply to negative reviews. ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tokenmetrics.com))
- Inconsistent grades: "they give two different values for the grades" (Dec 2024)
- Wrong data: "displays incorrect mcap, volume, and price data"
- Price: "every good feature is pricey" (Jul 2025)
- Broken bot: "The bot doesn't even work" (Aug 2025)
- Refunds: "I want my money back" (Feb 2026)
- Lifetime-plan dispute: a buyer says "lifetime Premium" no longer covers the new "Pulse" product (Sep 2026)
- BBB: 2 complaints in 3 years, not accredited. ([BBB](https://prod.bbb.org/us/tx/austin/profile/cryptocurrency-exchange/token-metrics-0825-1000193933/complaints))

**Regulatory**
- In 2022 the SEC sued CEO Ian Balina over an undisclosed bonus for promoting the Sparkster (SPRK) ICO. This was not about the ratings.
- In March 2025 Balina said the SEC would drop the case; I found no final order. ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/309816/token-metrics-ceo-ian-balina-says-sec-to-drop-its-case-against-him), [Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/crypto/cryptocurrency-analyst-was-paid-5-million-to-push-ico-sec-says))

**Languages**
- Not stated. It appears to be English only.

**Gaps for a Tunisian beginner**
- The closest conceptual competitor to Qirat's grades, but aimed at traders: hourly grades, moonshots, bots and a token.
- Grades are opaque, no public record of hits and misses.
- Prices are high or unclear.
- No Arabic or French.

## Track records and regulatory/legal history

**Verifiable, loss-inclusive track record**
- **None of the 11 publish a verifiable, loss-inclusive record of their ratings or signals.**
- Token Metrics, the closest analogue to Qirat's grades, makes outperformance claims with no audited period, benchmark or methodology. ([affiliated blog](https://dev.to/crypto-trader/how-token-metrics-users-beat-exchange-traders-with-ai-backed-indices-18h8))
- Its users report seeing two different grade values for the same coin. ([Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/tokenmetrics.com))
- The only loss-inclusive ledgers I found come from small, unknown signal sellers. They advertise hash-chained or recomputable logs, which shows the idea is marketable but rare. ([Product Hunt: ezath](https://www.producthunt.com/p/ezath-auditable-crypto-signals))

**Token Metrics**
- The SEC case against the CEO concerned undisclosed paid promotion of an ICO, not the ratings. The CEO said in March 2025 that the case would be dropped. ([Decrypt](https://decrypt.co/309816/token-metrics-ceo-ian-balina-says-sec-to-drop-its-case-against-him))

**CoinMarketCap**
- Private suit *Cox v. CoinMarketCap OpCo* (3:21-cv-08197) alleged CMC suppressed a rival token's ranking. ([Law360 case page](https://www.law360.com/cases/6140de10125ea902389834ed/articles))
- In February 2026, Binance.US and "a digital asset market data website" again beat a ranking-misstatement class action. I did not confirm the website was CMC. ([Law360](https://law360.com/articles/2442807))

**SEC, AI trading signals (relevant precedent)**
- On 2026-09-29 the SEC sued Cryptoaiml for marketing AI signals with "98% accuracy". About $12.5M was taken from 300+ retail investors; the dashboards showed fake profits and investors found their withdrawals frozen. ([InvestmentNews, Oct 2026](https://www.investmentnews.com/regulation-legal-compliance/fake-signals-frozen-accounts-sec-sues-crypto-ai-platform-over-125m/268436))
- In December 2025 the SEC sued a WhatsApp "investment club" scheme that promised AI-signal profits. ([Bloomberg Law](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/securities-law/fake-crypto-platforms-whatsapp-clubs-bilked-investors-sec-says))

**SEC, Titan (performance advertising)**
- Titan was fined $850K in 2023 for advertising hypothetical crypto returns of up to 2,700% "annualized" without material context. A second penalty followed. ([WealthManagement](https://www.wealthmanagement.com/regulation-compliance/sec-settles-first-charges-stemming-updated-marketing-rule), [WebProNews](https://www.webpronews.com/fintech-adviser-titan-hit-with-second-sec-penalty-over-misleading-2700-crypto-return-claims))

**UK FCA**
- Enforcement notices to 50+ crypto firms over unsubstantiated safety claims in ads. ([Blockworks](https://blockworks.co/news/uk-watchdog-clamps-down-on-crypto-firms-for-misleading-advertisements))

**Takeaway**
- Regulators punish accuracy and return claims without context, not neutral, disclosed grading.
- Qirat's "no public misses, no winners-only stats" rule is consistent with this. A private, complete, methodology-disclosed record would be defensible, provided it is published in full and not cherry-picked.

## Patterns and unmet needs

**1. Everyone is bolting on AI. Nobody does AI for beginners in Arabic.**
- CoinStats (Apr 2026 agent), CoinGecko (Gecko AI, Apr 2026 Market and Portfolio Insights), CMC (CMC AI, May 2025; portfolio "Analyze" coming) and Messari (Copilot) all ship AI.
- All of it is English-first, metered or paywalled, and pitched as research or trading power: deep research, backtesting, price prediction.
- None is a calm coach that explains "what does this mean for me" in Darija, Arabic or French.

**2. "Why is it moving?" is now standard.**
- Delta, CMC (30-minute refresh) and CoinGecko Market Insights all explain price moves.
- Qirat's daily "weather" is in the same category. Its edge has to be tone (calm, anti-FOMO) and language, not the feature itself.

**3. No one rates "is this safe for a beginner?"**
- The aggregators (CoinGecko, CMC) list 17k+ tokens and face constant "scam listing" and "paid ranking" accusations. ([CoinGecko Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/coingecko.com), [CMC Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/coinmarketcap.com))
- The graders score momentum or hype: Token Metrics Trader Grade, LunarCrush Galaxy Score.
- A **risk-first, small-universe grade** (Qirat's ~18 coins with Karat and Risk) is unclaimed territory.

**4. Track-record transparency is an open lane.**
- Zero incumbents publish a loss-inclusive record.
- The one rating-heavy competitor (Token Metrics) has trust problems: 2.4★, inconsistent grades, refund fights.
- An auditable methodology page plus a complete private log made available on request would differentiate Qirat without breaking the "no public misses" rule.

**5. Scam and impersonation protection is a real, unaddressed pain.**
- CMC's Trustpilot is dominated by victims of fake "CoinMarketCap" callers and fee-to-withdraw scams.
- The SEC's 2025–26 cases involve WhatsApp clubs and fake AI-signal platforms.
- This matches the earlier user research: scams were the top pain and WhatsApp is a key channel.
- A **scam check** (paste a link, number or "platform" name and get a verdict and a Darija explanation) has no direct equivalent among these 11.

**6. Billing anger is universal.**
- Auto-renew and trial complaints hit CoinStats, Delta, TradingView, LunarCrush and Token Metrics.
- A transparent, reminder-first, cancel-in-one-tap billing policy is cheap to build and a visible trust signal.
- Note: Santiment accepts crypto for annual plans. Tunisian card and payment constraints were not verified (search budget ran out).

**7. Portfolio tracking is commoditised and error-prone.**
- Every tracker draws complaints about wrong P&L or cost basis and lost transactions (CoinStats, Delta).
- For Qirat, a **lightweight manual "my coins" view graded by Karat and Risk** gives most of the value without exchange-sync liability. Full sync would pit Qirat against free incumbents with 86K–173K reviews.

**8. Paper trading exists, but only for traders.**
- TradingView's free paper trading is chart-centric and gives no coaching.
- A beginner "practice wallet" that applies Qirat grades and shows calm feedback ("you bought during a storm day") is unoccupied.

**9. Retail research is being abandoned upmarket.**
- Messari dropped retail plans (June 2026), Glassnode's cheapest plan is $49/mo and its free tier reportedly removed, and LunarCrush starts at $90/mo.
- The low-price beginner segment is being vacated, not crowded.

**10. Language gap**
- Only CMC (app), CoinGecko (web) and TradingView (web) offer Arabic, and none offer Darija.
- None of the AI products advertise Arabic answers.
- French is widely available (CoinStats, Delta, CMC, TradingView).
