Protect and teach beginners before anyone trades

A review of today's app, 30+ competitors, the evidence on AI trading agents, and Tunisian law, ending in a ranked roadmap and a phased plan. Written for Abdelkerim.

Proposal · nothing built yet Not legal or financial advice Sources in docs/strategy/research-2026-11/

0The short version

Your idea: accounts with holdings (or "here's $10, trade it for me"), and a team of AI agents that watch the market, research coins, follow news and politics, and give buy/sell actions at 24 hours, 1 week and 1 month, hoping for about 90% good trades.

Sources: Alpha Arena S1 via Protos/Forklog; Barton et al. 2026 (arXiv 2609.05663); Lopez-Lira & Tang v6 (next-day drift, before costs). The 55–58% signal decayed to a Sharpe of 1.2 in 2.5 years and loses money at 0.2% round-trip fees.

Finding

Nobody gets near 90%

Papers with huge numbers tested on dates the AI had already seen in training; once tested later or live, the edge mostly vanished (51–62% of the Sharpe lost).

Finding

90% always hides something

Memory of the past, moves too fast to trade, or "win small often, lose big rarely" bots. One short-volatility fund had 85%+ winning months, then lost 96% in a day.

Finding

"Trade my $10" is the riskiest idea

In Tunisia it combines four regulated things at once: buying crypto for residents, managing others' money, holding funds, AML duties. It is also the #1 scam script regulators describe.

Finding

Exchanges already sell it

Binance, Bybit, Bitget and Coinbase offer chat-to-trade AI for $0–30/month. None publishes user results, none speaks Darija, users carry 100% of losses.

What Qirat can win instead

Be the calm, neutral, Darija-speaking guide that protects beginners: a scam check (paste a WhatsApp pitch, link, token or platform), my money in dinars (a risk check of your own coins), practice with $10 of paper money with a coach that spots FOMO, and a short daily "what happened and why". Agents explain and protect; plain, testable rules decide and measure. Any trading strategy runs privately on paper and must pass gates before anyone sees it.

The honest target is not "90% wins" but better risk-adjusted results than simply holding Bitcoin: smaller drawdowns, a similar or better Sharpe, and probabilities that come true as often as they say.

5Recommended system: rules decide, agents explain and protect

Numbers never come from the LLM. Every forecast is logged before its outcome. Same grade for everyone. No custody, no execution, no leverage. Plain Python jobs on the existing crons; no agent framework needed.

Profiles (need accounts)

Telegram Login first (no passwords to store). Fields: language, level (short quiz), learning goal, optional holdings, and "my rules" written by the user ("never more than 10% in one coin", "no new buys in a storm"). Not collected: income, age, wealth or horizon, because collecting them makes outputs look "suitable for you", which is the legal definition of advice.

Practice with $10

  • A virtual 10 USDT (shown in TND) at live prices, 0.1% fees plus spread, spot only, labelled "Simulation, not real money".
  • Modes: my picks · weekly DCA · follow a rule (e.g. two highest karats, cash in a storm) · always compared with just holding BTC.
  • The coach scores behaviour, not luck: "you bought after a +25% day", "you sold in a −10% day", "you broke your own rule".
  • Private progress only. No leaderboard: it rewards gambling and looks like a signal group.
  • Add-ons: risk doctor on real holdings in TND, a historical DCA simulator, and later "your agent vs you" once a strategy passes the gates.

AI cost at about 1,000 users

Coach on Claude Haiku 5.5 ($0.10 / $0.50 per million tokens) and a shared daily brief on Sonnet 5.5 cost roughly $5–15 a month; data adds $0–35. A personalised brief would cost about $360/month on Sonnet and is legally riskier, so the brief stays shared. Before switching models, confirm the Anthropic key status (memory says it was blocked until 1 Aug 2026).

6Validation: backtest, private paper trading, then gates

Stage A · ~2 weeks

Backtest

Walk-forward 2021–26 with 0.1% fees + 0.1% slippage, dead coins included, every variant counted (deflated Sharpe). Compared with holding BTC, an equal-weight top 18 and a BTC 50-day rule. LLM parts excluded.

Stage B · 6+ months

Private paper log

Each call logged before the outcome in a new paper: namespace in track_record.py, scored at 7 and 30 days, reported only in the owner's weekly Telegram note.

Stage C · ~May 2027

Go / no-go

Pass: may appear inside Practice as an education rule. Fail: retired quietly, lesson goes to the coach. Real money stays out until a lawyer confirms a licensed path.

7Legal and cost constraints

Desk research, not legal advice. Crypto is not money in Tunisia; buying or holding it without BCT authorisation is treated as a foreign-exchange violation. The new Code des changes cleared committee in April 2026 but is not law, and would still require declaration and BCT authorisation. On 28 Oct 2026 the CMF named an unlicensed firm for "portfolio management presented as investment advice" on social media.

FeatureVerdictWhy
Education, impersonal grades, shared brief, scam check, botGoTalking about crypto isn't banned; impersonal, regular content is the safe side everywhere (MiCA, Lowe v. SEC)
Paper trading with virtual moneyGoNo funds or crypto change hands; label it a simulation
Self-reported holdings, user's own rulesGuardrailsPersonal data; AI may explain but never "sell your X"
Personalised buy/sell suggestionsNoThe textbook definition of advice (MiCA, UAE, US)
"Trade my $10 for me"Hard noFX law + third-party management + custody + AML at once
Pro revenue in crypto, RedotPay or Telegram Stars (TON)HoldThe operator acquiring crypto may need BCT authorisation. Safer: TND via local rails, or foreign cards into a Startup Act FX account. Lawyer first.

Guardrails: no trade verbs in AI output (prompt + output filter, refusals logged); disclaimers in all languages including "not licensed by the CMF or BCT"; no affiliate links; minimal personal data; AI answers kept 12 months. Don't target Algeria or Egypt, which ban crypto promotion. Data stack: $0–35/month (CoinGecko, CMC Basic, Binance data-api, Fear & Greed, DefiLlama, GoPlus, GDELT).

10Decisions for you

  1. Which phase first? Recommendation: Phase 0 + Phase 1 (Protect). Scam check is the strongest wedge, needs no accounts and feeds marketing. Alternative: Phase 3 (Practice with $10) if you want your idea first.
  2. Drop "trade my $10 for me" and personalised buy/sell calls, replaced by Practice mode and the private strategy lab.
  3. Payments: crypto, RedotPay and Stars revenue are flagged high risk for a Tunisian operator. Book a lawyer before January.
  4. Hide the public Track record tab to match "no public misses"; measurement continues privately.
